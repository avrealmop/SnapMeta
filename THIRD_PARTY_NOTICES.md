## Ghostscript

Ghostscript is used for EPS rasterization (converting EPS files to previews
before metadata generation). It is distributed bundled with SnapMeta in
`assets/gs-runtime.zip` and installed locally on first use.

- Project: GNU Ghostscript / Artifex Ghostscript
- Version bundled: 10.07.1
- Website: https://ghostscript.com
- License: **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0** (AGPL-3.0)
- License text: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.txt
- Source code: gs-10.07.1 source archive is available at
  https://github.com/ArtifexSoftware/ghostpdl/archive/refs/tags/gs10071.zip
  and from https://ghostscript.com/download.html

Ghostscript operates entirely locally and does not transmit any data over the
network.
